## Kesslernity

I publish free reference libraries for the agent platforms people are being asked to roll out, and I write down what breaks.

Two vendors are covered here, and the material is not a pitch for either. Where a claim comes from a vendor, the repository names the page and the date it was read.

### The rule that caps all of it

AI prepares, humans decide. Nothing in these libraries authorises a permit to work or a lockout, closes an incident, or signs off an inspection. Where a skill touches a decision like that, it drafts the paperwork and stops.

### When it is wrong

Before I publish a listing I have three models from different labs attack it, and I make the call. They do not catch everything. What got through is written up rather than quietly edited: [what broke, dated, with the fixes](https://www.kesslernity.com/what-broke?utm_source=github&utm_medium=profile&utm_campaign=gh_profile&utm_content=fail_line).
